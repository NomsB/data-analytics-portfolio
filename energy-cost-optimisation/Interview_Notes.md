# Interview Talking Points – Energy Cost Optimization Project

## 1. Why this project matters
Energy costs are a controllable operational expense. Understanding cost drivers enables targeted efficiency initiatives and informed decision-making.

## 2. Key technical skills demonstrated
- SQL joins and aggregations
- Star schema data modeling
- DAX measures for trend and anomaly analysis
- Power BI dashboard design

## 3. Analytical judgment
Defined high-cost days using defensible thresholds and reported findings honestly when no extreme outliers were present.

## 4. Business impact
Identified high-cost sites and peak periods, enabling prioritization of efficiency interventions.

## 5. What I would do next
Incorporate additional data such as weather patterns, tariff changes, and a longer time horizon to improve cost optimization insights.
