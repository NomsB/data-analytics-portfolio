# Executive KPI Dashboard & Business Performance Analysis

## Business Scenario
Executives need a high-level view of company performance to make strategic decisions. This project simulates an executive dashboard that tracks revenue, profit, customer growth, and operational efficiency metrics.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
This dashboard focuses on metrics commonly used by leadership:
- Total Revenue
- Profit Margin
- Monthly Sales Growth
- Customer Acquisition Trend
- Top Performing Products or Regions
- Underperforming Segments
