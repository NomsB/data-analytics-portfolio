I built an executive KPI dashboard in Power BI to simulate how leadership monitors business performance. 
I defined key financial and operational KPIs, analyzed trends and profitability, and highlighted underperforming segments. 
The goal was to translate raw transactional data into strategic insights for decision-making.

Expanded Key Insights
1. Revenue Growth Is Consistently Negative
  - The revenue growth trend remains in negative territory across most of the timeline, indicating sustained contraction rather than a short-term fluctuation.
  - **Business meaning:** The company is not just experiencing volatility, but a prolonged slowdown. Leadership should investigate demand drivers, competitive pressure, or pricing strategy.

2️. No High-Revenue / Low-Profit Imbalance
  - The scatter plot shows a strong positive relationship between total revenue and total profit across product categories. There are no categories generating high revenue while dragging down profit.
  - **Business meaning:** The product portfolio appears financially healthy. Sales growth is not being achieved at the expense of margin, which suggests pricing and cost structures are aligned.

3️. Profitability Is Volume-Driven
- Because profit increases proportionally with revenue across categories, margins appear relatively stable. There is no evidence of extreme margin variation between categories.
- **Business meaning:** Future profit growth depends more on increasing sales volume than on margin optimization alone.

4️. Lack of a “Standout” Growth Category
  - The scatter plot does not show one category dramatically outperforming others in profit relative to revenue.
  - **Business meaning:** Growth is distributed rather than driven by a single high-performing segment. This reduces risk but may also signal a lack of a strong growth engine the company can aggressively scale.

5️. Frequent Sharp Declines Suggest Instability
  - The revenue growth chart shows repeated sharp drops rather than smooth fluctuations.
  - **Business meaning:** This pattern could indicate:
      a. Heavy discounting cycles
      b. Irregular customer demand
      c. External market volatility

  - Leadership may need to stabilize demand through better forecasting, subscription models, or more consistent promotional strategies.

6️. Potential Data Cutoff or Structural Drop at the End
  - If the final period shows an especially deep decline, it may represent incomplete reporting rather than actual performance.
  - **Business meaning:** Executives should confirm whether the most recent month is fully recorded before reacting to the apparent downturn.

7️. Strategic Focus Area
  - Since margins are stable and no product is underperforming dramatically, the primary strategic challenge is revenue growth, not cost control.
  - **Executive takeaway:** The business model is stable, but growth momentum is weakening. Strategy should prioritize demand generation, market expansion, or product innovation rather than margin restructuring.
